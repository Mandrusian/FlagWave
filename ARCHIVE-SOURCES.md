# Historical and alternate flag artwork

62 designs. Labels distinguish national, civil, military, party and symbolic flags.
Categories and dates describe the selected design, not an endorsement or a ranking.
Symbolic ideology flags are representative designs; movements do not necessarily have one official flag.

Images are Wikimedia Commons renderings resized to fit 640 x 640 pixels and converted to 256-colour palette PNG.
No political design was added or removed based on endorsement. Country flags remain available separately.
Dates identify the design or usage period shown; historical flags can have multiple variants.

Each image retains its own license. CC BY-SA derivatives are distributed under the version stated below.
The source link provides the original file history and attribution details.
